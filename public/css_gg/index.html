GunGame Rules 

    GunGame ● NiDE.GG ● 100 Tick  -  css.nide.gg:28015

     How to play :

    • Team GunGame deathmatch: Counter-Terrorists against Terrorists, with no round end.
    • Everyone levels up alone: each kill with your current weapon gives you the next weapon of the list.
    • The first player to get a kill on the knife level wins the map.
    • A knife kill steals a level: you go up one level and your victim goes down one.
    • You respawn instantly at a random spot, with a short spawn protection that ends as soon as you fire.
    • Reserve ammo is infinite, just reload.
    • Killing yourself (falling, kill command) costs you one level.
    • Bots fill the server and leave as players join. A bot wins the map only after 3 winning kills on the last level, and knife or HE grenade kills on bots do not count while another player is in game.
    • Your level is kept if you reconnect, and late joiners do not start from the first weapon.
       

     Weapon order :

    Glock → USP → P228 → Desert Eagle → Five-SeveN → Dual Elites → M3 → XM1014 → TMP → MAC-10 → MP5 → UMP45 → P90 → Galil → FAMAS → AK-47 → Scout → M4A1 → SG 552 → AUG → M249 → AWP → SG 550 → G3SG1 → HE Grenade → Knife

    Type !weapons in game to see the order of the current map.
     

     End of the match :

    • After each match, a winner screen opens automatically, then the next map starts.
    • Every win counts in the server ranking (!top, !rank).
       

     Commands :

    Type them in chat with ! (or / to hide them from the chat).

    • !level Your current level and who is winning.
    • !weapons The weapon order.
    • !score The level of every player.
    • !leader The current leaders.
    • !top The top winners of the server (also !top10).
    • !rank Your place in the winners ranking.
    • !rules The GunGame rules and how to play.
    • !commands This command list, in game.
    • !nsfw Show or hide the sprays marked as NSFW ⁽¹⁾.
       

     Gameplay :

    • Camping the enemy spawn points to kill players as they respawn is prohibited, any attempt will result in a warn, a slay or a kick.
    • Boosting levels (letting a friend or a second account kill you, trading kills, feeding knife steals) will result in a ban.
    • Abusing map bugs or glitches (getting out of the map, unreachable spots) will result in a slay, a kick or a ban.
    • Do not stay AFK: AFK players are moved to spectators automatically.
