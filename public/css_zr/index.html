• Zombie Revival Rules 

     Gameplay :

    • It is not allowed to destroy, damage or push props and barricades that are being actively used by teammates.
      • Violations can be punished with a slay or a kick depening on the case.
    • Humans and zombies may have a ceasefire.
      • There is no obligation to shoot the zombie or infect the human.
         

     Communications :

    • Players with bad microphone quality can be muted without a warning.
    • No excessive spam in chat - violations may be punished with a gag.
    • No excessive spam in mic - violations may be punished with a mute.
    • Constantly interrupting someone trying to lead a map will result in a mute without warning.
    • No harassing of other players.
